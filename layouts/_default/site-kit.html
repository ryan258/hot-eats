04 / Build & maintain

Make it your own.

Choose a restaurant preset, keep the pieces you need, and replace the illustrative content with reviewed restaurant information.

Everyday commands
./he preview
./he preview --preset food-truck
./he check
./he today
./he menu
./he history

For the full owner-run verification suite: ./he test.

Create an independent restaurant
python3 scripts/sculpt.py --destination ../new-restaurant --name "New Restaurant" --slug new-restaurant --preset cafe

The copy retains the module and component library. Contacts, schedules, provider destinations, reviews, jobs, and approvals are reset. Retained examples still need editing and review.

Presets, content, and source files

Kitchen sink, diner, café, fine dining, fast casual, bar, bakery, food truck, multi-location, and Taco Lester’s (parody). Example datasets live in data/scenarios.json; client records live in data/restaurant.json.

Guide fixtures live in data/component_library.json. Reusable partials live in layouts/partials/components/, with shared styles in assets/css/components.css. Read docs/component-library.md for integration guidance.

Current client content status: {{ $data.meta.status }}.

Owner tools & publication

Local tools cover daily announcements, special hours, service pauses, menu updates, CSV editing, preview diffs, recovery snapshots, gathering briefs, recipe arithmetic, and private operational logs.

Read docs/owner-guide.md, docs/data-model.md, and docs/publication.md. Private records stay in .local/. Publication builds require reviewed client data and a real domain; they create a local artifact and do not deploy. Provider acceptance is a separate check.
