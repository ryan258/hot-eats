## 30-second introduction

> Hot Eats is a website kit for independent restaurants and the people who build their sites. Guests can find the menu, check the right hours and location, and see what to do next. The restaurant can start with a design that fits how it serves, and a web partner can configure the site around the restaurant’s own brand and service links.

## Sales questions

**Can we keep our booking or ordering provider?**  
The kit can link to the destination the restaurant approves for each configured location or service. The provider handles completion. We would review the specific destination and fallback with the restaurant before release.

**Who updates the menu and hours?**  
Assign a restaurant content owner and a web operator during scoping. The project has local tools for routine updates and recovery. A pilot can show what the restaurant can maintain directly and what belongs with its web partner.

**Can we keep our brand?**  
Yes. We can use a preset as a starting layout and adapt it to approved restaurant assets, typography, colors and content. Review the actual site with the restaurant before publishing.

**Can you build our site, host it, or support it after launch?**  
Those are separate scope decisions. A proposal should state who owns the source and domain, who publishes, who updates the site, and how support is arranged.

**How much does it cost?**  
A proposal should reflect the approved design, content migration, locations and services, provider checks, handoff and ongoing responsibilities. Share a price only after those terms are set.

## Proposal starter

Copy the block below into a new client proposal. Keep the decision and dates in the same record.

> **Website pilot for [Restaurant]**  
> **Guest task.** [The question or task to improve.]  
> **Scope.** Adapt [preset] for [location]. Prepare [menu, hours, arrival information and confirmed action].  
> **Restaurant provides.** Brand assets and permissions, current facts, service destinations, and an approver.  
> **Web operator provides.** Source setup, preview, review corrections, update rehearsal, and delivery of the agreed artifact.  
> **Acceptance.** [Named guest tasks, device/keyboard/print checks, provider confirmation and update rehearsal.]  
> **Handoff.** [Source ownership, hosting and publishing responsibilities, update owner, support terms and recovery path.]  
> **Fee and timing.** [Approved terms and milestones.]  
> **Next decision.** [Decision] by [date].

## Media copy

**Fact sheet.** Hot Eats is a configurable restaurant website kit built on Hugo. It includes nine illustrative restaurant presets, reusable page modules and components, and local tools for maintaining selected restaurant facts. Example businesses show possible designs and workflows. Service links can be configured for approved third-party destinations.

**Short listing.** Hot Eats helps independent restaurants and their web partners build a branded site for the menu, the right hours and location, and the next step.

**Image caption.** Hot Eats preview showing [preset and guest task]. Restaurant name, prices, schedules, and service information are illustrative.

**Assets.** Use the [logo family and social artwork](../../assets/brand/) and follow the [visual identity guide](03-visual-identity.md). Use the real build for a screenshot, label the example, and get permission before showing any client business or footage.

Before pitching a public story, add the approved announcement date, spokesperson, press contact, product URL, and any authorized client quotes or pilot results.
