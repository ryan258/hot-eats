# Brand operations and release checklist

Use [the kit index](README.md) as the handoff. Keep drafts and approved client evidence with the person responsible for updating them.

## Source of truth

| Material | Source and review owner |
| --- | --- |
| Product promise, audience, and offer | [Strategy](01-strategy.md). The product owner approves changes. |
| Product copy and claims | [Voice and messaging](02-voice-and-messaging.md). The writer checks each claim against the actual demo or approved pilot evidence. |
| Product mark, palette, and artwork | [Editable assets](../../assets/brand/) and [visual standards](03-visual-identity.md). The brand owner reviews the specific use, contrast, and export. |
| Guest components and restaurant examples | [Living guide](../component-library.md). The web operator checks routes and states. Restaurant facts belong to the relevant restaurant reviewer. |
| Launch, measurement, and learnings | [Launch playbook](05-marketing-and-launch.md) and [SWOT](06-market-and-swot.md). The pilot owner records what happened, when, and what changes. |

## Review in one pass

- **Identity.** Use the right logo and first-use descriptor. Confirm name, domain, and handles for the release context.
- **Content.** Check the audience, the one action, the action destination, and the exact scope of every claim. Label examples. Verify names, quotes, facts, imagery permissions, and captions.
- **Design.** Check reading order, legibility, meaningful alt text, keyboard focus, contrast, small screens, and the intended print or export size.
- **Commercial terms.** Approve public availability, the offer, pricing, rights, hosting, update and support responsibilities, and contact ownership before using them in a pitch or campaign.
- **Restaurant readiness.** Follow the [publication checks](../publication.md) for an actual restaurant. Obtain restaurant content sign-off and acceptance of each configured service handoff before release.
- **Approval.** Record the material, revision, audience, reviewer, decision, unresolved evidence, and destination. The owner approves external use.

## Make changes easy to resume

Use a single line for routine review notes. For example, “One-sheet · Nov 12 · Owner review · Confirm pilot contact.” Keep reusable copy in the guide, with campaign or client-specific variations in their own working files. Update the live brand preview and any collateral affected by a token, logo, or positioning change. Refresh competitor claims from primary sources before reusing them externally.

Run `./he preview` to review the product brand and restaurant examples together. The brand routes and their served assets are preview material. For a restaurant release, the standard publication build and its manifest are the applicable artifact. See [verification scope](../verification.md).
